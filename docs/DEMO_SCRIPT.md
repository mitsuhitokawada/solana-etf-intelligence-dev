# English demo / pitch script — approximately 90 seconds

Solana ETF Intelligence connects institutional flows with onchain activity.

Today, ETF data and Solana network data live in separate dashboards. Analysts have to combine them manually. Our prototype brings them together and makes the same evidence available to ChatGPT through MCP.

Click Fetch live data. We retrieve SOL price and history from CoinGecko, US Solana ETF flows from SoSoValue, DEX volume from DefiLlama, and a recent performance sample directly from Solana mainnet RPC.

The executive summary highlights the latest observations. Price history supports seven, thirty and ninety-day views. ETF bars show inflows and outflows.

The relationship section matches ETF trading dates to UTC daily SOL returns and shows the sample period, number of observations and Pearson correlation. This is a descriptive association, not a claim that ETF flows cause price movements.

The installed ChatGPT Plugin exposes this same snapshot through our hosted MCP server. Ask ChatGPT to retrieve the data and explain the relationship, citing sources and limitations. If the global message composer is unavailable, we provide a copyable prompt.

We target analysts and research teams who need a traceable view of institutional demand and Solana activity. Next steps are better trading-session alignment, longer licensed history and reliable conversational interaction.

This is a live-data development version. Our original submitted demo uses simulated data and remains preserved.
